Best Lassagna Ever!

This is the best lassagna recipe that you'll need. Some back story about my family recipe and reminisce about childhood memories. Joke about being the lassagna potluck person. This recipe resulted in my marriage. My kids love it. Amazing.

Ingredients

    Ragu

  • 2 lbs ground pork
  • 1 lb beef chuck (20%)
  • koscher salt, fresh ground pepper
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 oz. pancetta/bacon
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 celery, finely chopped
  • 1 medium carrot, peeled, finely chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 3/4 cup dry white wine
  • 1 28-oz can whole peeled tomatoes
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 cup whole milk

    • Bechamel + Assembly

  • 7 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/4 cup plus 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 6 cups whole milk
  • 4 oz grated parmesan cheese
  • pinch of cayenne
  • pinch of ground nutmeg
  • koscher salt, fresh ground pepper
  • 1 1/4 lb dried lassagna noodles
  • extra-virgin olive oil for greasing

    Steps

  1. "Preheat oven to 225F. Premix the ground pork + beef chuck. Season with salt + pepper then form into meatballs."
  2. "Add 2 Tbsp to a large dutch oven and heat up. Cook the meatballs in 2 batches for about 6 minutes each until browned all around. Transfer to a container or sheet tray."
  3. "Reduce heat and cook pancetta until beginning to crisp (about 5 minutes). Add onions, carrots, celery and garlic and stir occassionally until softened (about 6 minutes). Then add tomato paste and stir it in as well and cook for another 2 minutes."
  4. "Add in 3/4 cup of white wine and stir and reduce until completely evaporated. Add in the tomatoes and crush with your hands. Cook until jammy and reduced (about 8-10 mins) then add the chicken broth, whole milk, and meatballs. Bring to a simmer then cover pot and transfer it to the oven. Check it every hour or so to make sure it's still simmering until 3-4 hrs."
  5. Smash em and reduce and simmer if needed. Storable in the freezer up to 3 months

    6. Beschamel + Assembly

  6. Melt 7 Tbsp of unsalted butter then whisk in the flour until the mixture starts to smell slightly nutty (about 4 minutes). Whisk in 6 cups of whole milk and reduce and whisk until mixture is smooth and velvetty. Then add in the parmesan, cayenne, and nutmeg. Salt and pepper as needed"
  7. Preheat oven to 375F. Cook the lassagna noodles in salted water for about 3 minutes. Noodles should soften, but still snap in half and not just bend. Set aside in a bowl of cool water.
  8. Coat at 13x9" pan with olive oil. Layer the pan with ragu, noodle then bechamel creating 5-6 layers of pasta and ending with a cup of bechamel.
  9. Cover pan lightly with aluminum foil and bake until edges are boiling (about 1 hr). Then remove and increase oven temp to 425F. Uncover and place back on the top rack once oven is to temp for about 10-15 minutes when top is browned nicely. Take it out then let it sit and cool before serving.
