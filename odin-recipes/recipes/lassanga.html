Best Lassagna Ever!

This is the best lassagna recipe that you'll need. Some back story about my family recipe and reminisce about childhood memories. Joke about being the lassagna potluck person. This recipe resulted in my marriage. My kids love it. Amazing.

Ingredients

Ragu

  • 2 lbs ground pork
  • 1 lb beef chuck (20%)
  • koscher salt, fresh ground pepper
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 oz. pancetta/bacon
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 celery, finely chopped
  • 1 medium carrot, peeled, finely chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 3/4 cup dry white wine
  • 1 28-oz can whole peeled tomatoes
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 cup whole milk

Bechamel + Assembly

  • 7 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/4 cup plus 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 6 cups whole milk
  • 4 oz grated parmesan cheese
  • pinch of cayenne
  • pinch of ground nutmeg
  • koscher salt, fresh ground pepper
  • 1 1/4 lb dried lassagna noodles
  • extra-virgin olive oil for greasing
